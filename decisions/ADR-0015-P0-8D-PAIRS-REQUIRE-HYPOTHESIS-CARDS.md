# ADR-0015: A P0-8C Pair Is Not an Executable Interaction Hypothesis

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-20
- Scope: P0-8D0 design and any later P0-8D activation

## Context

P0-8C froze 12 cross-archetype representative pairs with no hard redundancy edge. Its handoff contains factor IDs and a structure-only rationale, but no interaction form, formula, sign, state threshold, or falsification rule. The first P0-8D V1 disposition spec therefore had zero executable definitions and was not activated. The one-time Alpha Factory activation exception is closed and aborted in governance audit commit `ce2b776a2f17b492a7be3d40a08a0ba863d8019b`.

## Decision

Treat those 12 pairs as an **investigation set**, not an interaction execution registry. P0-8D0 examined all 12 using only frozen structure evidence and factor-card mechanisms, retained every considered conceptual form and negative/deferred disposition, and issued zero executable Interaction Cards. The current P0-8D V2 proposal has zero authorized interactions and attempts and must not be activated.

A later executable interaction requires one pre-result mechanism-supported form, exact formula, direction, state/threshold where applicable, parent and simple-addition comparators, timing, coverage, incremental endpoint, failure rule, and independent review. Cross-cluster complementarity, standalone performance, and A+B improvement do not by themselves establish state dependence. A deferred pair cannot be tested under multiple forms to choose the best after results. The 12-pair ceiling and inherited search accounting never reset.

## Evidence and limits

- `EMPIRICAL_RESEARCH_ONLY_SELECTION_CONTAMINATED`: P0-8C's 27-factor/351-pair structure, 16 archetypes, graph, and residual tests; handoff hash `2502ac870d0767db8839665ca664251e7652b7e493e7c6986719f993486ccb4c` and result hash `995efd57f952c9ddfb5527f992225764bb7ce22814f7a28478492f66e04e9c12`.
- `DOCUMENTED`: parent factor-card hypotheses, P0-8D0's 12-row design audit, interaction policy, timing, preprocessing, and evaluation gates.
- `UNKNOWN`: whether any genuine interaction exists, because no P0-8D interaction was executed.
- `SYNTHETIC`: any future modeled trading-cost evidence, which does not yet exist for interactions.

## Consequences

Alpha Factory remains in the direction-only state. No P0-8D V1 or V2 interaction execution, WF4/WF5 selection, 2026 access, ML, dynamic allocation, P0-8E, Alpha Pool promotion, or production claim follows from this decision. A controller may separately choose a new pre-result mechanism-design task or an archetype-only path, with its own exact directive and gates.

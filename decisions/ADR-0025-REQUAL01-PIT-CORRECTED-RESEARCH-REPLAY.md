# ADR-0025: REQUAL01 PIT-corrected research replay

- Status: Accepted for `REQUAL01_PIT_CORRECTED_RESEARCH_REPLAY_V1`
- Date: 2026-09-21
- Controller directive: `ALPHA-REQUAL01-PIT-CORRECTED-RESEARCH-REPLAY-V1`

PITPRICE01 established `UPSTREAM_MODEL_INVARIANT=NO` and left Ridge F1/S123 superseded for forward selection. This decision authorizes one bounded correctness requalification under additive execution namespaces. It reuses exactly the frozen P0-8A 97-factor eligible population, P0-8B WF1-WF3 rules, P0-8C structure rules, CAD01 grammar/queue algorithm/seed/budgets, CAD02 equivalent small-system and null-calibration freedom with a hard timeout, and the fixed MODEL01 family/config roster.

The corrected data version must implement PITPRICE01 causal analytical prices and label-end total-return targets without overwriting `FREE_DAILY_V1`. Old P0-8B, P0-8C, CAD01, CAD02 and MODEL01 artifacts remain historical and read-only. The replay carries all prior selection history and can produce only `SELECTION_CONTAMINATED_RESEARCH`; it is not independent confirmation.

The task does not authorize P0-8D interaction experiments, Exposure wealth replay, Portfolio sizing selection, 2024–2025 or 2026 market access, payment, broker action, or real trading. Exact 50k cash/share wealth remains blocked on separately directed CAQUAL01 entitlement capability.

Evidence classes: causal bounded market computations are `EMPIRICAL_RESEARCH_ONLY_SELECTION_CONTAMINATED`; cost and null calibration diagnostics are `SYNTHETIC`; production, independent validity and exact cash/share execution remain `UNKNOWN`.

## Model budget for P0-8F

- Generation-1 `P07C_ML_ENET_A010_L50`: fixed comparator.
- Ridge: maximum one predeclared configuration.
- ElasticNet: maximum two predeclared configurations.
- LightGBM: zero.
- Transformer/deep learning/RL: zero.
- Input set: only frozen P0-8C representatives plus accepted P0-8D interactions; no direct 100+ raw-factor model.

The exact Ridge/ElasticNet penalties must be frozen in the P0-8F spec before results. Training-only preprocessing and chronological folds remain mandatory.

## Boundary after P0-8G

P0-8G may prepare a new calendar-only holdout adequacy decision but cannot access or consume 2026. A later one-run protected evaluation requires an exact directive, exact frozen entity, exact primary endpoint, immutable pre-access manifest, and no tuning/rerun.

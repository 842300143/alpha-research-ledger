# CAD_RESEARCH_CONTRACT_V1

Task `CAD01_COMBINATORIAL_DISCOVERY_PILOT_V1` is `EXPLORATORY_SEARCH`. The exact machine-readable policy is `configs/cad01_combinatorial_discovery_v1.json`. This document states the interpretation and gates that a future resume worker must recover. The old P0-8D/P0-8D0 12 pairs remain 12 investigated, zero executable, and unrun; CAD01 has a separate namespace and cumulative ledger.

## Objective and claims

Generate small expressions over the frozen 16 P0-8C representatives and test whether they improve a fixed model's paired forward prediction, then check a small shortlist under unchanged synthetic trading costs. OII is this project's shorthand for **model-relative increment**: the change from a baseline predictor to the same predictor plus a candidate, fitted only on earlier labels, evaluated on matched future rows. It is not a standard newly invented statistic, a conditional-mutual-information estimate, or a causal effect. A formula can improve the representation available to a model without adding any new external raw field. A candidate can be an `EMPIRICAL_HYPOTHESIS` without a unique financial story; no causal explanation may be invented from its observed result.

Three reported layers are separate: representation novelty (identity, constant, value equality, rank equivalence, and near correlation), matched model increment, and matched economic increment after synthetic costs. Correlation does not by itself erase an incremental test. Subtraction and parent aliases are treated according to their actual numeric representation. Direction or sign variants consume distinct proposal slots. The baseline is ridge regression on all 16 centered same-date ranked representatives, with alpha 1.0 and training-only standardization. The enhanced fit has the same penalty and preprocessing plus one candidate. No model or hyperparameter is selected from this pilot's outcomes.

## Data and time

The raw `FREE_DAILY_V1` files must be predicate-filtered to `trade_date <= 20231229` before any Arrow-to-pandas conversion. Only the 72-symbol frozen action-aware subset is used. Signal-date cross-sectional ranks use the contemporaneously observed eligible cross-section after `t` close. Entry is no earlier than `t+1` open. The primary 20-session median-relative label ends at the actual `exit_date_20`, never merely an assumed offset. A training row is valid only when that date is at or before the fold's training end; a test row is valid only within its fold and at or before its fold end. Warmup prior to signal evaluation is allowed within the 2019–2023 boundary. Overlapping labels are retained for estimation but uncertainty is not described as independent observations. The 2024–2025 Generation-2 test tranche was used earlier by Generation-1 and is locked here. The 2026 protected holdout is sealed. Both are denied before load; no label may be completed with a 2024 row.

The actual 16-feature complete-case baseline sample, not the 3,207-security dataset size, is the denominator for matched coverage. A date needs at least 60 names and 60% of its reference rows. A candidate needs at least 95% of valid baseline evaluation rows. `FREE_DAILY_V1` is non-PIT-certified and historically survivorship-incomplete; all outcomes remain `SELECTION_CONTAMINATED_RESEARCH`.

## Grammar and schedule

Terminals are exactly the 16 raw frozen representative signal definitions after the frozen same-date centered-rank view. No old retrospective residual is a terminal. The interpreter whitelist is `add`, `subtract`, `multiply`, `abs`, `rank`, and a fixed `gate_ge_zero` using the other operand's centered rank threshold zero. Division is excluded from V1. Expressions have at most three distinct bases, depth three, and 15 total nodes. There is no arbitrary Python evaluation, generated code installation, network operation, or filesystem operation in expression evaluation. Canonical IDs sort commutative operands, retain parent lineage, and record semantic and numeric duplicate reasons separately.

Deterministic stratified enumeration uses seed 20260920 and the form quotas frozen in the JSON contract. The first 32 evaluation slots explicitly include direct three-factor products, independent of parent and pair scores; this is a fixed 20% diversity allocation in the 160-slot pilot schedule. The maximum beam width is 32 but this pilot uses full predetermined order, so no outcome-driven beam promotion. All proposals including illegal and duplicate ones are retained. The first 160 legal novel scheduled expressions may reach labels, subject to stop rules. Low-order comparators that compute new outcome metrics also count toward the 256 ceiling. No failed slot is recycled.

## Prediction and cost gates

The primary statistic is the mean across dates of `RankIC(pred_baseline_plus_candidate, y) - RankIC(pred_baseline, y)` on exactly matched rows. Preserve each date, fold mean, valid rows, and diagnostic uncertainty. A candidate screen requires mean delta at least 0.005, at least two positive folds, and at least 95% matched coverage. These are engineering screening thresholds, not p-values or a statistical error guarantee. A candidate involving two bases is also compared with parents and fixed single-variable nonlinear controls; a three-base expression is compared with parents and fixed pair products. If only a unary transform improves, classify it as such. Candidate complexity, stability, redundancy, and costs are separate outputs.

Only up to ten screened, nonduplicate expressions receive a `TOP20_EQUAL_WEIGHT` research vector diagnostic. Baseline and challenger use identical dates, rows, starting cash, 20-session rebalance, 100-share rounding, commission, tax, and slippage. Report start/end dates, fold and cumulative decimal returns, and whether costs were subtracted; repeat at 2x costs. These modeled fills are not real orders. Zero, all-missing, constant, all-tie, and exact-duplicate scores have explicit statuses. An arbitrary ticker-order holding can earn market return but is never candidate-attributable Alpha.

## Budget and stopping

Hard maxima: 2,048 proposals, 256 new unique label-evaluated expressions, three folds per expression, ten cost rechecks, five archived candidates, and four hours cumulative **real search** wall time. Default concurrency is one and memory target is 4 GiB; batching can reduce memory without changing the research rule. Save append-only attempt/proposal lineage and a checkpoint every 16 evaluations. Resume inherits all counters, failures, elapsed search time, queue position, and seed. Stop at any hard maximum, any fatal integrity/resource condition, or after at least 128 evaluations when the last 64 add no qualified nonduplicate and the best paired delta improves by less than 0.001. Stop means this grammar and budget ended, not all price-volume patterns were exhausted. No automatic budget increase is permitted.

# Alpha Factory Vision

## Goal

Build an automated Alpha Research Factory with a Portfolio Engine and an Execution Engine.

The system is not a single-strategy project. It should continuously turn data and research ideas into controlled experiments, preserve the evidence, combine sufficiently independent signals, and translate them into feasible China-market portfolios and execution plans.

## Durable outcome

- A reliable, point-in-time and survivorship-aware research substrate.
- A repeatable discovery process for formula, ML, and later Agent/LLM or text/event alphas.
- An evaluation discipline that emphasizes robustness and limits overfitting.
- A portfolio process that combines low-correlated alphas after risk and cost.
- An execution process that models market rules before Shadow and later broker integration.

## Repository split

The research ledger controls intent and preserves knowledge. Alpha Factory executes code, pipelines, experiments, and results. Stable IDs and immutable references join the two planes.

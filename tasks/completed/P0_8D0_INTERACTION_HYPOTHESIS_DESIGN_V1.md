## Scope and method

Investigate exactly the 12 ordered pairs in the P0-8C handoff. Parent definitions come from the frozen P0-8A factor cards. All parents are P0-8C representatives. The P0-8C Factor Graph gives every listed pair `relations=[]`: cross-cluster status means no hard redundancy edge, not proof of an interaction. Its residual tests fit a target against a *set* of earlier representatives without labels in the fit; they measure residual distinctness, not the change in A's predictive effect across B states. Previously frozen P0-8B metrics were used only for the already-declared breadth constraint: the nine relevant parents' WF1-WF3 minimum valid symbol counts are 64–71. No new score, label, RankIC, IC, return, coverage probe, or interaction result was computed.

Conceptual forms considered were the policy's centered-rank product (`N`), A conditional on a fixed or training-fitted B state (`C`), label-free cross-fitted residual conditioned by B (`R`), and fixed rank addition (`+`). Listing alternatives is a design audit, **not** registering them for testing. `+` is a comparator or a simple combination, never evidence of true effect modification. A stock-level median/tercile B state would typically leave far fewer than 60 names per date in this approximately 72-name research subset. The inherited primary gate requires at least 60 securities and 60% reference-row coverage per sufficient fold; a regime cell also needs at least 20 daily observations and the factor's minimum breadth. **This is a provisional structural feasibility warning, not a measured conditional-coverage result or a declaration that every conditional design is impossible.** A market-wide B gate could preserve breadth but would change the stock-level parent semantics and risk creating a strategy timing rule, outside this design. No threshold was chosen.

## Quality gate and accounting

An `EXECUTABLE` card needs: defined parents and mechanism; one form; exact signed formula; exact direction; fixed or training-only state and threshold if applicable; A, B, and addition comparators; unchanged after-`t`-close/t+1 timing; credible minimum coverage; frozen primary and incremental endpoints, fold, cost, redundancy, and rejection rules; and a falsifiable difference from simple addition. **Zero of 12** satisfy the full conjunction without imposing an arbitrary form, directional sign, or selective stock-state threshold. No Interaction Card is therefore issued and there is no executable P0-8D hypothesis.

Design outcomes: 12 investigated; 0 executable; 4 `SIMPLE_COMBINATION_ONLY` (01, 05, 06, 10); 2 `NO_DEFENSIBLE_HYPOTHESIS` (09, 11); 6 `AMBIGUOUS_DEFERRED` (02, 03, 04, 07, 08, 12). Six are rejected from the current interaction execution population and six are deferred; none may be converted using results. No candidate was added, replaced, backtested, or promoted. The 12 pair inspections and the conceptual alternatives are new **research design degrees of freedom**, not 12 executed predictive hypotheses. The P0-8B 97 definitions/291 fold attempts, P0-8C 351 pair assessments/26 residual decisions, and all P0-7 history remain cumulative. The 36 maximum interaction fold slots remain unused and non-recyclable in this design.

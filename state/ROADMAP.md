# Roadmap

## Near term

1. `P0_6E_FREE_DATA_EXPANSION_V1`: `COMPLETE` / `P0_6E_PASS_WITH_WARNINGS`.
2. `P0_7C_LONG_HORIZON_ALPHA_RESEARCH_V1`: `COMPLETE` / `P0_7C_PASS_WITH_WARNINGS`.
3. `P0_7D_ALPHA_POOL_QUALIFICATION_V1`: `DESIGN_READY` / `NOT_YET_EXECUTED`.
4. On controller direction, qualify the exact 17 frozen P0-7C OOS survivors under one predeclared redundancy and incremental-contribution procedure. Do not add candidates or access 2026.
5. Use P0-7D's retained positive and negative evidence to design a narrow `P0_7E_ALPHA_RESEARCH_V2`; do not begin broad Generation-2 search implicitly.

## Generation-2 research directions

- Separate the liquidity mechanisms behind turnover persistence, liquidity shock, and low-turnover behavior.
- Distinguish short- and medium-horizon reversal from liquidity and microstructure effects.
- Test LowVol robustness against action, PIT, survivorship, and tradability limitations.
- Decompose ElasticNet scores to determine what is genuinely incremental to transparent Formula survivors.
- Predeclare a small number of cross-family interactions only when P0-7D identifies a missing mechanism.
- Require any complex portfolio constructor to beat top-20 equal weight on matched frozen score streams after costs.

These are research questions, not an authorized parameter grid or execution task.

## Later

- Consider a separately designed, hash-bound, one-run protected-holdout evaluation only after candidate, pool, code, costs, and comparison rules are frozen and the partial 2026 interval is judged adequate without peeking.
- Revisit paid-data validation only when a quantified P0-7D or Generation-2 limitation cannot be resolved on the free route.
- Use Shadow execution before any later broker integration.
- Paper trading and real trading remain later and require separate execution and risk controls.

## Protected boundary

The 2026-01-05 through 2026-09-15 holdout remains `UNACCESSED / UNCONSUMED` throughout P0-7D and Generation-2 research under ADR-0010. Neither holdout may be consumed through roadmap work without a separate exact controller directive and pre-access one-run manifest.

# Research Principles

1. **Free first.** Do not pay for data or LLM APIs until free work reveals a specific bottleneck whose removal has credible incremental value.
2. **Pools over miracles.** Seek weak but predictive, stable, low-correlated, post-cost usable signals; do not optimize around one magic strategy.
3. **Economic mechanism first.** A signal needs an economic intuition, plausible market mechanism, falsification criteria, and known confounders.
4. **Simple before complex.** Use transparent baselines and simple ML before deep models. Complexity must earn its place out of sample and after costs.
5. **Control researcher degrees of freedom.** Predefine splits, metrics, costs, comparison sets, and multiple-testing treatment.
6. **Protect Blind.** Treat protected Blind evaluation as a scarce, controlled, non-repeatable resource.
7. **Model China-market reality.** Incorporate PIT and survivorship concerns, T+1, price limits, fees, slippage, liquidity, and fills.
8. **Separate evidence types.** Keep `EMPIRICAL`, `DOCUMENTED`, `SYNTHETIC`, and `UNKNOWN` distinct.
9. **Preserve negative knowledge.** Failed ideas and negative results reduce future duplication and must remain discoverable.
10. **Trace every decision.** Connect papers and ideas to hypotheses, experiments, execution tasks, results, reviews, and decisions.
